III —

Simple, and yours

Nothing to install

No app, no sign-up with us. Folio works in any modern browser — computer, tablet or phone.

Your login stays on your device

When you connect an account, your login is saved on this device only — you can review it any time, or remove it with one press.

Nothing to pay

Folio is free and open source — for everyone, forever.

How your accounts work
There is nothing to unlock or set up. Connect the account you already use at school and your books appear automatically. You can forget an account at any time — its saved login is erased from this device.
